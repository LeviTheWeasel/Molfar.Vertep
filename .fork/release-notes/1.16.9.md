# ⏱️ Molfar Vertep 1.16.9

The fix the 1.16.8 note pointed to. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then update Roleplay to 4.135.2 (Settings > Updates).

## The error
"plugin roleplay__engine route POST /chats/<id>/send failed: The script execution has exceeded the maximum allowed time limit". A phone reporter on 1.16.8 sent the note that now ends this error: **"ran 10.2 s, engine CPU 10.4 s, script stood still 1.1 s: the plugin's own work took this long"**. The phone did not pause the engine; the plugin was working and stopped at **10 s**, while the Roleplay plugin asks for 30 s.

## Why 1.16.3 and 1.16.7 did not help
Installing or updating an app from its repository marks every plugin in it as "imported", and the 30 s were given only to "local" plugins. Both fixes were checked on a plugin that had not been installed that way, so they never reached the Roleplay people actually run, on a phone or on a computer.

## What changed
- **A plugin's own limit is honoured whatever its origin** (`limits.executionMs`, 10 to 30 s). It applies to its routes (a send, opening the app) and now to its **hooks** too: the Dashboard, Litopys, World DM and Agents each add their part to every reply in one, and on a phone one of them could stop at 10 s.
- An imported plugin gets nothing new it could not already take: it could already ask for up to 12 passes of 10 s.
- **Roleplay 4.135.2** asks for 30 s in those four plugins as well (until now only its chat engine did).

## Known limits
- 30 s is the ceiling. A phone ran this send for 10 s of real CPU. A bigger chat, or a slower phone, can still pass 30 s. The note in the error will say so, and the next step would be to make the prompt pass lighter.
- Not tried on a phone yet.

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
